## Script and scenes

| Scene ID | Narration or silent text | Visual change and teaching purpose | Evidence | Result-reading hold |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| s01 | Opening question and concrete input | Establish the subject and input | Source or example | Time to inspect the input |
| s02 | Explain the causal step | Show cause, state change, consequence | Source or trace event | Time to understand the result |
| s03 | Change one condition and resolve the question | Compare outcome; optional prediction pause | Verified second case | Time to read the answer |

Add, combine, or remove scenes to fit the subject. These are roles, not a mandatory three-scene film.
